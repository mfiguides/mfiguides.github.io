Why you need it

The hard part usually isn't the divorce. It's what happens after.

Conflict shapes your child's outcome

Research points to ongoing conflict, not the divorce itself, as the deciding factor. This guide shows what is in your control to reduce it.

02

Your messages set the tone

Learn the BIFF method: brief, informative, friendly, firm. Short replies that don't feed the argument, with a worksheet to practise on a real message.

Compare five common custody schedules, then score them against your commute, work hours and your child's age.
